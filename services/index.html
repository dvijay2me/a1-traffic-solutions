What we provide

Nine ways we turn footage into decisions

Every service below is built on the same accurate, video-based data pipeline, classified by vehicle or movement type and delivered as a clean, ready-to-use report.

Intersection counts

A1 Traffic Solutions has expertise in analysing intersections along with multiple vehicle classifications to support signal-requirement decisions, processing intersection counts with large volumes of data.

Roundabout counts

Roundabout junctions are circular junctions with multiple arms used to avoid traffic signal requirements. We have the expertise to track the movement of vehicles passing through any roundabout.

Pedestrian & bicycle counts

We have very good experience counting bicycle turning movements. Our results give accurate data for pedestrian and bicycle movements at roads, parks, schools, public places and store entrances.

Parking surveys

We process all types of videos into a highly accurate data report — known as Midblock Counts or Link Counts — measuring total vehicle volume for each vehicle classification.

Punctuality surveys

We understand how the punctuality survey plays a significant role in transport planning, so our dedicated team is keen to provide accurate arrival or departure times for all vehicle types at platforms & airports.

Queue length surveys

Queue length surveys are done at certain sections to study traffic behaviour. We provide queue length data lane-wise with expected minutes of intervals.

Road volume counts

We process all types of videos into a highly accurate data report. We produce Road Volume data reports known as Midblock Counts or Link Counts, measuring total vehicle volume by classification.

People counting system

We closely analyse people counting in retail and shopping malls to help decide sales performance and business outcomes by analysing customer traffic.
